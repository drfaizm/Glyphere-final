Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A typical custom typeface project takes 4–8 weeks depending on complexity, number of weights, and glyph coverage. Simple logo type can be completed in 1–2 weeks.

We deliver production-ready OTF, TTF, and WOFF2 files. Variable font formats are available for Enterprise licenses. All files include proper hinting for optimal screen rendering.

Our standard licenses do not allow modification of the font files. However, we offer a Font Modification service where we can make changes on your behalf at a discounted rate.

Due to the digital nature of fonts, we do not offer refunds after download. For custom projects, a 50% deposit is required upfront with the balance due upon completion.

Enterprise licensing includes unlimited installations, brand exclusivity options, variable font support, dedicated design support, and priority access to revisions and new weights.
