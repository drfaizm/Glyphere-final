Data Transparency

Cookie & Storage Policy

We believe in complete data clarity. Learn about the cookies, local storage variables, and web technologies we rely on to deliver an optimized foundry storefront.

Like most digital type foundries, Glyphere uses standard cookies and browser local storage technologies to secure account access, remember font specimens you add to your shopping cart, remember preferred grid layouts, and run anonymous site analytics.

Below, you can explore the exact cookies we utilize, review their specific purpose, and adjust your consent preferences dynamically.

Consent Preferences

Manage and configure your active cookie preferences below. Functional technologies are mandatory for standard website operations.

Functional Variables Mandatory

Required to process shopping cart contents, active user sessions, font testing resets, and layout preference keys.

Analytical Metrics Optional

Anonymously tracks website load performance, page view counts, and popular custom font specimens to help us improve foundry craft.

Personalisation & Promotion Optional

Enables targeted social promotions, tailored newsletter recommendations, and unique designer-behind-the-type showcase features.
